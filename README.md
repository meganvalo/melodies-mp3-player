# melodies.

A private, offline-friendly MP3 player that lives in your browser and installs on your home screen like a real app. No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your music stays on your device.

It's a single web page, so there's nothing to download from an app store.

## Features

- **Play your own MP3s:** add songs from your device and they're saved inside the app
- **Library, Favorites, Playlists, and Queue** tabs
- **Search** your songs by name
- **Favorites:** tap the heart on any song
- **Playlists:** create, rename, and delete them; add songs from inside a playlist (**+ Add songs to this playlist**) or from any song's ⋯ menu
- **Play next and Add to queue:** build a queue on the fly
- **Drag to reorder** songs in the Library, a playlist, or the Queue (grab the ≡ handle)
- **Rename songs:** fix a song's displayed name from its ⋯ menu
- **Shuffle and repeat:** true shuffle (every song plays once before any repeats, and Previous steps back through what played), a **Shuffle all** button, and repeat that cycles through off, repeat all, and repeat one
- **Playback speed:** 0.75×, 1×, 1.25×, 1.5×, and 2×
- **Picks up where you left off:** remembers your last song and position, and reopens in the same playlist, Favorites, or Library you were playing from
- **Remembers your settings:** repeat, shuffle, speed, and theme stick after you close the app
- **Lock screen controls:** play, pause, next, and previous from the lock screen and Control Center, where your device supports it
- **Themes:** Dark (default), Cream, Peach, Mint, and Deep Teal, using the Sweet Summer color palette. The opening screen follows your theme too
- **Opening screen:** a splash with the "m." logo that matches your theme
- **Settings:** themes, resume and auto-continue toggles, library size, and storage protection
- **Backup and restore:** save everything (songs, playlists, favorites, settings) as one .zip file and restore it later
- **Import and export playlists:** move playlists and favorites between devices as a small file
- **Share a song:** send or save a song's file from its ⋯ menu
- **Select several songs** to add them to a playlist, favorite them, queue them, or delete them in one go
- **Duplicate warning:** you're told before adding songs that are already in your library
- **Trim silence:** cut silence from the start or end of a song during playback, with auto-detect
- **Auto dark mode:** switch to a night theme with your phone's appearance or on a schedule
- **Mini player:** a slim player bar stays at the bottom while you scroll your list
- **Song info and album art:** titles, artists, albums, and cover art are read from your MP3 files, shown in the list and on the lock screen, and searchable
- **Sorting:** sort the Library and Favorites by name, artist, or date added
- **Smart lists:** automatic Recently added, Recently played, and Most played lists
- **Position memory:** long songs (over 10 minutes) resume where you left off
- **Queue tools:** clear the queue or save it as a playlist
- **Edit song info:** change a song's title, artist, album, and cover art
- **Undo:** take back removed songs, deleted playlists, cleared queues, and more for a few seconds
- **Skip ±15 seconds and bookmarks:** jump around long tracks and save spots to return to
- **Wide layout:** a two-column layout on iPad and computers, plus keyboard shortcuts

## Install it on your device

Open the app's web address in your browser first, then follow the steps for your device.

### iPhone and iPad (Safari)

1. Open the app's link in **Safari**. Installing only works from Safari on iOS.
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.
4. Open **melodies.** from your home screen.

This is the main way the app is designed to be used.

### Android (Chrome)

1. Open the app's link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu.
3. Tap **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).

### Computer (Chrome, Edge, or Safari)

- **Chrome or Edge:** click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open the browser menu and choose **Install melodies.**
- **Safari on Mac:** choose **File → Add to Dock**.

You can also use it without installing, just by keeping the page open in a browser tab.

## Using it

### Adding music

1. In the **Library** tab, tap **Add songs**.
2. Pick one or more audio files, such as MP3s, from your Files app or wherever they're stored.
3. They appear in your Library. Tap a song to play it.

Tip: if your music is in iCloud Drive, you can pick it straight from there. Songs are copied into the app, so you only need to add each one once per device.

### Playing

- Tap a song to play it. When it ends, the next song in that list plays automatically. If the list runs out, playback carries on down your Library.
- Use the ring and seek bar to jump around in a song.
- The speed button (for example, **1×**) cycles through the playback speeds.
- The heart under the controls favorites the current song.

### Song options (⋯)

Tap the ⋯ next to any song for:

- **Play next:** put it at the front of the queue
- **Add to queue:** put it at the end of the queue
- **Add to playlist…:** add it to an existing playlist or a new one
- **Rename:** change the name shown in the app
- **Edit info…:** change the title, artist, album, and cover art
- **Remove from this playlist:** only when you're inside a playlist
- **Remove from library:** delete it from the app

Renaming only changes the name shown in melodies. Your original files are never touched. Removing a song deletes only the copy stored inside the app.

### Editing song info

Tap ⋯ → **Edit info…** to change a song's title, artist, and album, or to choose your own cover image (**Choose cover**) or remove the current one. Edits are stored in the app, not inside the MP3 file, and they're included in backups. Edited songs are skipped by **Rescan song info**, so your changes aren't overwritten.

### Undo

Removing songs (one or many), removing songs from a playlist, deleting a playlist, clearing the queue, and deleting a bookmark all show an **Undo** button for about 9 seconds. Undoing a removed song brings back its place in your Library, its playlists, favorite, play history, saved position, trim, and bookmarks. If you close the app during that window, the removal still goes through. **Delete all songs & playlists** in Settings does not have undo and asks first.

### Skip and bookmarks

- **−15 / +15** under the player jump back or forward 15 seconds.
- The **flag** button opens bookmarks for the current song. Tap **+ Add bookmark here** to save the spot, tap a bookmark to jump to it, ✎ to label it (for example "Chapter 3"), and ✕ to delete it.
- Bookmarks are saved in your backups.

The lock screen still shows previous/next track buttons. The ±15 buttons are inside the app.

### Bigger screens and keyboard shortcuts

On iPad and computers (about 820 pixels wide or more), the player stays on the left while your library, playlists, and queue fill the right. Phones keep the single column. With a keyboard: **Space** plays or pauses, **←** and **→** skip 15 seconds, and **B** adds a bookmark at the current spot.

### Shuffle

- Tap the shuffle button (the crossed arrows) to turn shuffle on or off. Shuffle plays every song in the current list once, in random order, before anything repeats. **Previous** steps back through what already played.
- With shuffle on and **Repeat all** on, it keeps going and reshuffles after each full pass. With repeat off, it stops after one pass.
- **Shuffle all** (next to **Add songs** in the Library) starts shuffling your whole library from a random song. In a playlist, tap **Shuffle** next to **Play**.
- Songs you queue with **Play next** or **Add to queue** still play first, and shuffle carries on afterward.
- **Spread out artists when shuffling** (Settings → Playback, on by default) avoids playing two songs by the same artist back to back when it can. Songs without an artist aren't affected.

### Reopening where you left off

melodies. remembers which list you were playing from. If you were listening to a song in a playlist (or Favorites, a smart list, or the Library) and you close the app, reopening it shows that same list with your song highlighted, and playback continues through that list. If shuffle was on, the shuffle order is kept too. Tap play to continue. This needs **Resume where I left off** (Settings → Playback) to be on. If the playlist was deleted in the meantime, it falls back to the Library.

### Sorting

In the Library and Favorites, tap **Sort** (next to Select) and choose **Manual order**, **Name A–Z**, **Artist A–Z**, or **Recently added**. Songs without an artist go last when sorting by artist. Drag-to-reorder only works in Manual order. Songs play through in the order you see.

### Smart lists

In the **Playlists** tab, the **Smart lists** section has **Recently added** (your newest songs), **Recently played**, and **Most played**. They update on their own, show up to 30 songs, and can't be edited. A song counts as played after about 30 seconds (or half of a short song). Clear the history in Settings → Playback.

### Remembering your place in long songs

Songs longer than 10 minutes, like DJ sets or audiobooks, remember where you stopped and pick up there next time ("Resuming from …"). To start over, tap ⋯ → **Play from the beginning**. You can turn this off in Settings → Playback.

### Queue

The **Queue** tab has **Clear** to empty the queue and **Save as playlist** to turn what's queued into a playlist (it asks for a name).

### Mini player

When you scroll past the main player, a slim bar appears at the bottom with the current song, play/pause, and next. Tap the song to jump back to the full player.

### Song info and album art

melodies. reads the tags inside your MP3 files (ID3 tags): title, artist, album, and cover art. Songs show their cover and artist in the list, the cover appears on the spinning disc and on the lock screen, and search matches artist and album names too. Songs without tags look as they did before.

- New songs are named from their title tag when it exists. You can turn this off in Settings → Library → **Use song info for names**.
- **Rename songs from their info** applies tag titles to songs already in your library (it asks first).
- **Rescan song info** reads the tags again.
- Only MP3 (ID3) tags are supported for now. Other formats still play, but without tag info.

### Duplicates

If you pick songs that are already in your library (same file name or name and size), melodies. tells you and lets you **skip duplicates**, **add everything anyway**, or cancel.

### Selecting several songs

In the Library, Favorites, or inside a playlist, tap **Select** next to the search box. Tap songs to check them (or **All**), then use the bar at the bottom:

- **Playlist:** add them to a playlist (or a new one)
- **Favorite / Unfavorite**
- **Queue:** play them next or add them to the end of the queue
- **Delete** (from the Library) or **Remove** (from a playlist)

Tap **Done** to leave select mode.

### Trimming silence

Tap ⋯ next to a song and choose **Trim silence…**.

- **Auto-detect silence** finds quiet stretches at the start and end for you (it can't analyze very large files, over about 60 MB).
- Use the **−1, −.1, +.1, +1** buttons to fine-tune how much to cut from the start and the end.
- **Hear start** and **Hear end** let you check the cut before you save.
- **Save trim** applies it. Trims only affect playback, so your original file is never changed. They're included in your backups. To undo, open **Edit trim…** and choose **Reset**.

### Settings

Tap the sliders icon at the top right. From there you can:

- Pick a **theme**. Your choice is remembered.
- Turn on **Auto dark mode** to switch to a night theme (Dark or Deep Teal) either with your phone's appearance setting or between times you choose.
- Choose an **app icon** (see below).
- Turn **Resume where I left off** and **Continue into Library when a list ends** on or off.
- See how many songs you have and how much space they use.
- Ask the browser to **protect your library** from being cleared when the device is low on space (support varies by browser).
- Back up, restore, import, export, or delete your library (see below).

### Backup, restore, import, and export

**Back up everything.** Settings → **Back up everything** builds one `.zip` file that includes the music itself, plus your playlists, favorites, and settings. The app doesn't set a size limit, and Settings shows the estimated size next to the button. When it's ready, tap **Save or share file** and save it to Files, iCloud Drive, or send it to another device. You can also open the zip on a computer to get your MP3s.

**Restore from backup.** Settings → **Restore from backup** and pick a backup file. Restoring **replaces** what's in the app right now, and you'll be asked to confirm. If the file can't be read, your current library is left untouched.

**Export playlists & favorites.** Saves just your playlists and favorites as a small file (no audio).

**Import playlists & favorites.** Reads one of those files and adds its playlists and favorites to what you already have. Songs are matched by name, so add the songs to the device first. Anything that doesn't match is skipped and reported.

### Changing the app icon

Settings → **App icon** lists the five icons (Dark, Cream, Peach, Mint, Deep Teal). iPhone only reads an app's icon at the moment it's added to the Home Screen, so an installed app's icon can't be changed in place. To switch:

1. Make a backup (Settings → **Back up everything**) and save the file.
2. Tap the icon you want under **App icon** to copy its link.
3. Paste the link into **Safari** and open it.
4. Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**.
5. Open the new copy, go to Settings → **Restore from backup**, and pick your backup file.
6. Delete the old copy. Deleting it erases the songs stored in that copy, which is why you back up first.

The new copy has its own separate library, which is why the restore step is needed.

**Share a single song.** Tap ⋯ next to a song and choose **Share or save file**.

Large libraries make large backups. Building one can take a while, and how well very big files share from a phone depends on iOS.

## Good to know

- **Your library is stored on each device separately.** Songs, favorites, playlists, and your queue don't sync between devices. If you use melodies. on two devices, you'll need to add your songs to each one, or move everything over with a backup and restore.
- **The data lives in your browser's storage.** Clearing website data for the site, or deleting and reinstalling the app on some devices, can erase your library. Keep your original MP3s somewhere safe, and make a backup from Settings now and then.
- **It works offline.** After the first visit, the app opens without an internet connection, and your saved songs play offline.
- **Background playback can vary.** Music keeps playing when you lock your phone, but how reliably it behaves depends on your device and browser. iPhone is the most-tested setup.
- **Which files work:** the app plays any audio format your browser can play. MP3 is the safest choice.

## Updating and troubleshooting

- **The app looks old after an update:** fully close it (swipe it away from the app switcher) and open it again.
- **Still not updating:** deleting the app from your home screen and adding it again usually works, but on iPhone it also erases the songs, playlists, and favorites saved inside the app. Make a backup in Settings first, and only do this as a last resort.
- **The launch screen looks outdated:** iPhone saves launch images when an app is added, so only a freshly added copy picks up new ones.
- **Launch screen color:** the brief screen iPhone shows before the app loads is a fixed black image. Once the app loads, the opening splash uses your theme.
- **Changing the app icon:** see "Changing the app icon" above.
- **A song won't play:** the file may be in a format your browser can't play. Try converting it to MP3.
- **Music stops at the end of the list:** turn on repeat all (tap the repeat button until you see "Repeat all").

## What's inside

There's no build step or server. The app is a few static files:

| File | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `index.html` | The whole app: layout, styling, logic, and embedded font |
| `sw.js` | Service worker that makes the app work offline |
| `manifest.webmanifest` | Lets the app be installed with a name and icon |
| `icon-*.png` | App icons |
| `splash-*.png` | iPhone launch screen images |

It uses standard browser features: IndexedDB and localStorage for saving your data, the Media Session API for lock screen controls, and a service worker for offline use.
